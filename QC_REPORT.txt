BROWSER-SMOKE NOTE
------------------
The container's headless Chromium --dump-dom process does not terminate reliably (including with the background update disabled), so it was not used as a release gate. Browser-specific logic that can be isolated from the DOM is directly executed under Node during QC: browser normalization, packed-snapshot inflation and materialization. The complete final inline script is also syntax-checked.
