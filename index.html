Special Score UP is modeled independently from Active Skills as magnitude × duration ÷ song length. The combo-biased model rewards stronger Specials in later positions. SAR multiplies H/M/L probabilities during its Special window; because exact chart slots are not supplied, the optimizer averages that boost across the song by SAR% × duration ÷ song length.

Model assumptions and limitations
  • Base Active probabilities: H = 55%, M = 46%, L = 37%. These are treated as fixed in-game values and are not user-editable.
  • Active timing: a card first checks after one full interval; checks repeat every interval; each successful Active lasts its listed duration. Timing is evaluated on a one-second grid.
  • Active overlap: only the highest Active Score UP is credited at a time. Equal magnitudes resolve left-to-right.
  • Active conditions: LIFE, combo and team-composition conditions written on Active Skills are assumed satisfied.
  • Score Support: passive and Outfit Support add together and multiply the winning Active Score UP only. They do not increase direct Special Score UP.
  • Passive activation and targeting: “2 X” means at least two eligible team members. Non-self stat/Support passives target the two eligible cards with highest Base Total; ties resolve left-to-right. Self-only passives affect their owner.
  • Outfits: Outfit conditions are checked using the five team members. An external sixth card may supply the Outfit Skill, but its owner does not count toward conditions or provide a passive.
  • Ordinary Specials: every member activates once, left-to-right, in five non-overlapping chart slots. Direct Score UP is normalized as magnitude × duration ÷ song length. Combo-biased mode uses slot weights 0.92 / 0.96 / 1.00 / 1.04 / 1.08 as a general proxy for increasing combo value; it does not use a specific chart’s note density.
  • SAR: SAR is assumed teamwide, its written condition is assumed satisfied, and it multiplies the base H/M/L probability rather than adding percentage points. With no song-specific Special timestamps, effective probability is approximated as base probability × [1 + Σ(SAR% × duration ÷ song length)], capped at 100%. Exact alignment between SAR windows and Active checks is not modeled.
  • Card pool: only 5-star cards are included. 4-star and 3-star cards are outside the current database and cannot appear in optimizer results.
  • Score model: “Other score bonus” is added directly. The Stat × Score result is a comparative index, not the game’s literal Unit Score; board, memory, member power-up and exact per-note/combo scoring are not included.
  • Interpretation: rankings are model outputs, not guarantees. Small score gaps should be treated as uncertain, and updates to the game or newly discovered mechanics may change the results.
  • Owned-card searches: when “Use only cards I own” is enabled, all five team cards—and any external or specifically selected Outfit card—must be marked as owned. The oshi is automatically included. At least five different holomems must remain available after exclusions.
  • Roster rule: normal and Summer versions of the same holomem cannot coexist in the five-member team.

Methodology, terminology and mathematical definitions Model documentation

This section states what each term means and reproduces the calculations used by the optimizer. It describes the model—not the game’s proprietary internal formula. All calculations use unrounded values internally; the results table rounds values only for display.

Important: Stat × Score is a comparative model index. It is not labeled or presented as in-game Unit Score because Holomem Board Bonuses, Memory Bonuses, Member Power-Up Bonuses, exact chart note density, exact combo progression, hidden rounding, and possibly other mechanics are missing.
1. Units, symbols and percentage conventions
i ∈ {1,2,3,4,5}: team position, from left to right.
t ∈ {1,…,L}: one-second timeline point; L is the selected song length in seconds.
Pi, Ti, Si: card i’s base Performance, Technique and Sense.
Bi: card i’s Base Total, used for top-two passive targeting.
mi, di, Ii: Active magnitude, duration and interval.
pi0: fixed base Active probability: H = 0.55, M = 0.46, L = 0.37.
ri: total passive Score Support assigned to card i, written as a decimal.
δO: Outfit trigger indicator; 1 when its condition is met, otherwise 0.
Bi = Pi + Ti + Si

Stat and Support percentages are stored as decimal multipliers: +41% = 0.41 and +11% = 0.11. Active/Special magnitudes and the displayed score-bonus outputs are percentage points: an Active magnitude of 120 means +120% Score UP.

2. Eligibility, passive activation and top-two targeting

A card is eligible for a requirement when its Type exactly matches the requirement or its Generation text contains the requirement. A “2 X” passive or Outfit condition triggers when at least two of the five cards are eligible.

N(c) = ∑i=15 𝟙{card i is eligible for condition c};    triggered ⇔ N(c) ≥ 2

For a non-self passive, the recipients are the two eligible cards with greatest Base Total Bi. Equal Base Totals resolve left-to-right. A self-only passive affects its owner, but only after the same two-card condition has triggered.

Top2(c) = the first two cards after sorting eligible cards by (−Bi, position i).

Normal and Summer versions of the same holomem are treated as the same member identity and cannot coexist in the five-card team.

3. Team stat

Let bi,P, bi,T, bi,S be the summed targeted passive boosts to Performance, Technique and Sense, and bi,A the summed “All Stats” passive boost. Passive bonuses are additive against each card’s base stats.

Statpassive = ∑i=15 [Pi(1+bi,P+bi,A) + Ti(1+bi,T+bi,A) + Si(1+bi,S+bi,A)]

Let oP, oT, oS and oA be the selected Outfit’s Performance, Technique, Sense and All-Stats fractions. When triggered, the Outfit is added from the team’s base stat sums, not multiplied onto already passive-buffed stats.

Team Stat = Statpassive + δO[(oP+oA)∑Pi + (oT+oA)∑Ti + (oS+oA)∑Si]

This is the value shown as Team stat. It excludes Holomem Board, Memory and Member Power-Up bonuses.

4. Active timing, priority and effective probability

The model uses a one-second grid. Card i is in a potential Active window at second t when:

ai(t) = 𝟙{t ≥ Ii and (t mod Ii) < di}

This places the first check after one complete interval and repeats the same duration window every interval. Written LIFE, combo and team-composition Active conditions are assumed satisfied.

If Specials are enabled, the teamwide approximate SAR multiplier is:

ρ = 1 + ∑k∈SAR qk · min(Dk, L)L

Here qk is SAR percentage as a decimal and Dk is that Special’s duration. Each card’s effective Active probability is:

pi = min(1, pi0 · ρ)

SAR therefore multiplies the fixed H/M/L rate. Multiple duration-weighted SAR exposures add inside one team multiplier. This is an across-song approximation: only checks inside real SAR windows should be boosted in-game, but exact chart timestamps are not supplied to the optimizer.

Active priority is greater magnitude first. Equal magnitudes resolve left-to-right. Let Hi be the set of cards with higher priority than card i. Assuming activation events from different cards are independent, the probability that card i is the credited winner at second t is:

wi(t) = ai(t)pi ∏j∈Hi[1 − aj(t)pj]
5. Raw Active, Coverage and Score Support

Raw Active is the song-average expected magnitude of the highest credited Active:

Araw = 1L ∑t=1L∑i=15 miwi(t)

Coverage is the song-average probability that at least one Active is running:

C = 1L ∑t=1L{1 − ∏i=15[1 − ai(t)pi]}

The code evaluates the same quantities efficiently with intersection counts and inclusion–exclusion. For a non-empty set A of cards, define:

N(A) = ∑t=1L ∏i∈Aai(t)
Araw = 1L ∑i=15mipi∑A⊆Hi(−1)|A|(∏j∈Apj)N(A∪{i})
C = 1L ∑∅≠A⊆{1,…,5}(−1)|A|+1(∏i∈Api)N(A)

Passive Score Support is recipient-specific. If card i receives total passive Support ri, then:

Apassive = 1L ∑t=1L∑i=15mi(1+ri)wi(t)

Let oR be the selected Outfit’s teamwide Support fraction. The displayed Adjusted Active is:

Aadjusted = Apassive + δOoRAraw
Support uplift = Aadjusted − Araw

Score Support affects the winning Active Score UP only. It does not increase direct Special Score UP.

6. Ordinary Specials and combo-biased slot weights

Each card’s direct-score Special is normalized across the chosen reference song length. Let Mi be Special magnitude, Di its duration, and γi its position weight.

Speciali = MiDiγiL
Special bonus = ∑i=15Speciali

Combo-biased mode uses:

(γ1,γ2,γ3,γ4,γ5) = (0.92, 0.96, 1.00, 1.04, 1.08)

Neutral mode uses γi=1 for every position. The weights average to 1, so they redistribute value toward later positions without changing the five-slot mean weight. They are a general proxy for later combo value, not measurements from a specific chart. “Ignore Specials and SAR” sets Special bonus to 0 and ρ to 1.

7. Total bonus and Stat × Score

Let O be the user-entered Other score bonus in percentage points. It is not populated automatically from Board, Memory or Member Power-Up systems.

Total bonus = Aadjusted + Special bonus + O
Stat × Score = Team Stat · [1 + Total bonus100]

Stat × Score is the objective maximized by the optimizer. It is useful for ranking teams under the same assumptions but is not the game’s displayed Unit Score.

9. Outfit selection modes
  • Best outfit from the selected five: the optimizer tests the five team cards’ Outfit Skills and uses the highest-index result.
  • Best outfit from any card: the optimizer tests every distinct permitted Outfit Skill in the database. The winning Outfit may come from a sixth card.
  • Use a specific outfit: the chosen Outfit is forced, and the result also reports the best permitted alternative and its penalty.
  • When “Use only cards I own” is enabled, an external or forced Outfit card must also be marked as owned.
10. Search procedure and optimality limitation

The optimizer enumerates all eligible five-card compositions after the oshi, ownership and exclusion filters, and rejects duplicate member identities. Raw unrestricted combination count is:

Number of raw global compositions = C(n,5) = n!5!(n−5)!

To keep a browser-only global search practical, not every composition receives the full exact 120-order calculation immediately.

  1. Screen: every valid composition receives a quick score. The quick Active approximation uses each card’s scheduled uptime Ui independently and does not suppress overlapping weaker Actives:
Aquick = ∑i=15mipiUiL

The quick stage still applies passive stats, passive Support, Outfit testing, SAR and Specials, but it is a shortlist heuristic rather than the final score.

  1. First shortlist: for R requested results, global mode retains K1=min(50,000,max(30,000,1200R)) quick-scored compositions.
  2. Refinement: those compositions are tested with the exact overlap model in six representative orders; the best K2=min(8,000,max(5,000,250R)) continue.
  3. Final order optimization: each remaining composition is evaluated across all 5! = 120 team orders, and the best R rows are returned.

Oshi mode similarly keeps a shortlist Koshi=min(12,000,max(5,000,250R)) before final order optimization. If full-order optimization is disabled, the oshi remains in position 1 and only the other four cards are permuted.

Optimality caveat: every valid composition is scanned, but the full exact model is applied only to shortlisted compositions. Therefore, the returned “best” team is the best found by this documented multi-stage search—not a formal mathematical proof that no screened-out team could rank higher under the exact model.
11. What the model deliberately does not calculate
  • Holomem Board Bonuses, Memory Bonuses and Member Power-Up Bonuses.
  • 4-star and 3-star cards.
  • Exact per-note density, exact combo multipliers, chart-specific Special timestamps or difficulty-specific score curves.
  • Exact SAR-to-Active-check alignment; SAR is duration-averaged across the song.
  • Failure of LIFE, combo or composition conditions written on Active/Special skills; conditions are assumed satisfied where the database supplies the higher magnitude.
  • Undocumented game mechanics, hidden precision, future balance changes or data-entry errors.
